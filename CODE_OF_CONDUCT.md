# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge differing experiences.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or confidential information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces, as well as when representing the project publicly. Report
unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer using the email address in
`CITATION.cff`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Project
maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently exclude participants when necessary.
